Draft notes for the release after 0.6.1. The version number is not decided yet.

## New

- Share a save: *Saves* in the launcher (*Export Save*, *Import a Save*) and *Saves* on the Game tab of the settings
  menu write your save into one zip in your Downloads folder, with the game's log and settings for a bug report, and
  import a zip someone shared. Attach the bug report zip to a report from a place in the game, so it can be reached
  and checked quickly. An import copies your current save to the `saves` folder of the game folder first; in the game
  it takes effect at the next start.
- Customize Launcher (on the launcher's home screen): six colour schemes, a background picture of your own with
  adjustable dimming, animations (a gliding cursor, sliding screens, drifting sparks) and sound effects: the game's
  own select and back sounds, read from your game, quiet (Low) unless you pick another loudness or turn them off.
- Three more settings take effect without a restart: HDR between off and the tone mapper (only the game's native
  HDR still waits for one), the camera's stick sensitivity, and the speakers (stereo or 5.1).

## Fixed

- Ground, walls and characters that appeared and disappeared, black or unlit frames, and stalls of up to two seconds
  wherever something was shown for the first time (a new area, a cutscene). The graphics driver's cache had lost the
  shader libraries prepared at install, so every new pipeline compiled during play: after a driver update, or after
  an update of NX2navi that changes how the libraries are built (this one does on AMD RX 7000 and RX 9000 cards,
  where it also ends the 20 second start). The game now checks the driver's cache at every start and fills it again in the background by
  itself, an update fills it again before you play, draws wait a moment for a pipeline that only needs linking
  instead of being left out, and lighting passes are no longer left out at all.
- A black or unlit frame, or a light or a piece of ground missing for a frame, when something new came on screen (a
  hit, a new effect, a new enemy). A draw whose pipeline was not ready in time was left out of that frame, a lighting
  pass among them once its 50 ms were spent, and the frame was shown without it. Such a frame is no longer shown:
  the previous picture stays for the frame or two it takes, while the game runs on (`frame_hold_ms`, 120 by default;
  0 shows every frame as before).
- The performance overlay's VRAM line counted every program on the graphics card as if it were the game. It now
  shows the game's own share beside it (*game 6.8 GB* of 10.4 GB in use, in one measurement).
- Textures out of view for 11 seconds at 165 fps (30 seconds were meant) could show a blank stand-in for a few frames
  when they came back while memory was short. The wait is now two minutes of real time at any frame rate.
- Settings menu, mouse: a setting changes only on the two framed arrows beside its value, and the arrows stay in one
  place. A click anywhere on a row used to step the setting, and the left arrow moved with the value's width, so
  clicking through the values landed on the row and stepped the wrong way. Holding the button on an arrow repeats.
